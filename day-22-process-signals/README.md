## What I practiced

I practiced controlling Linux processes using signals and shell job-control operations. This included terminating processes, sending specific signals, bringing jobs to the foreground and background, suspending processes, keeping processes running after logout, and removing jobs from the shell's job table.

## What surprised me

What surprised me was how Linux provides different ways to control running and background processes depending on whether I want to stop, suspend, resume, or keep a process running.
