## What I practiced

I practiced the fundamentals of the Vim text editor, including opening files, entering and leaving insert mode, saving and quitting, deleting and copying lines, pasting content, and undoing or redoing changes.

## What stood out to me

What stood out to me was understanding that Vim uses different modes for editing and navigating text. Learning how to switch between these modes made it easier to understand how Vim can be used efficiently from the Linux terminal.
