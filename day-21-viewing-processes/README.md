# Day 21: Viewing Processes

Phase 5 - Process & Service Management | Day 21 of 30

Refer to [commands.md](./commands.md) for the 10 commands practiced today, including their syntax and my own explanation of what each command does and when I would use it.

## What I practiced

I practiced viewing and inspecting running processes in Linux using commands such as `ps`, `top`, `htop`, `pgrep`, `pstree`, `lsof`, `jobs`, and `nice / renice`. The practice focused on finding running processes, identifying process IDs, viewing process relationships, checking processes using ports, and working with process priority.

## What surprised me

What surprised me was seeing how many processes were running in the background, even when I was not actively using them.
